Medical Disclaimer

IMPORTANT NOTICE: Health Haven is an acquisition, marketing, and dashboard data aggregation portal. We do not directly provide emergency services, acute primary care, or urgent medical interventions.

This platform serves to connect individuals and corporate cohorts to licensed practitioners registered under the Botswana Health Professions Council (BHPC). Your reliance on any diagnostic indices or vital threshold recommendations aggregated by Health Haven is strictly for proactive prevention purposes. Do not bypass or delay calling your primary doctor, cardiologist, endocrinologist, or Botswana emergency services (+267 997 / 998) in an acute cardiovascular or metabolic event.

Biometric Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Health Haven, we understand that biometric data—including resting heart rates, continuous blood glucose monitoring, weight dynamics, and sleep records—constitutes the highest tier of sensitive personal data. This privacy policy defines how we safeguard your information.

1. Aggregation Protocols

We only aggregate health metrics that you explicitly consent to link. If you connect biometric sensors (such as Withings scales, blood pressure monitors, or activity trackers) to the Health Haven portal, your data is isolated on our secure cloud nodes before transfer. All data transmission uses end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption protocols.

2. Zero Third-Party Monetization

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your medical history, diagnostics, or biometric telemetry with any third party. This includes third-party advertisers, market researchers, and underwriters. For B2B corporate wellness cohorts, individual health charts are strictly hidden from HR managers and employer administrators; employers only receive aggregated, anonymized demographic reports to measure overall workforce ROI and chronic disease prevalence.

3. Biometric Encryption & Storage

All gathered medical files and biometric feeds are stored on ISO 27001 certified database shards using AES-256 military-grade encryption at rest. Keys are isolated in hardware security modules (HSMs) separate from client-facing application code.

Terms of Service

Last Updated: May 2026

1. The 30-Day Free Trial

Health Haven offers a 30-day trial for self-selected wellness packages (Mompati, Botsogo, Mooki). During this trial phase, you have access to continuous vitals logging and standard app features. Specialist clinician remote video consultations (as provided on the Mooki and Motlamedi packages) may be subjected to availability limits or initial co-pays during the trial, as governed by local insurance schedules.

2. Cancellation and Package Shifts

You can cancel your subscription at any time via your user dashboard. Because we use a unified clinical data layer (Breeze CDM), cancelling your active package does not delete your historical clinical charts unless you explicitly request a permanent purge. If you choose to resume service or shift to a new package, your existing medical history immediately reconnects, preventing critical medical data fragmentation.

3. User Conduct & Verification

You agree to provide true, accurate, and current biometrics. Falsifying clinical vitals or identity to bypass clinical triage triggers is a violation of these terms and may result in immediate account suspension.

DPA 2024 Compliance Statement

Botswana Data Protection Act (2024) Alignment

Health Haven operates in full compliance with the Botswana Data Protection Act, 2024 (DPA 2024). We have implemented robust structures to respect your rights as a data subject in Gaborone, Francistown, and throughout the SADC region.

1. Key Protection Frameworks

  • Section 74 Cross-Border Data Flows: All biometric and patient files are hosted locally within secure Botswana cloud nodes, or transferred exclusively to jurisdictions that meet or exceed DPA 2024 data privacy safeguards.
  • 72-Hour Breach Notification: In the highly unlikely event of a security anomaly, Health Haven maintains automated incident response protocols to notify the Botswana Information Commissioner and affected users within 72 hours of detection.
  • Data Minimization: We only process personal credentials and biological vitals strictly necessary to deliver your chosen wellness package.

2. Data Protection Officer (DPO) Contact

For any inquiries regarding your right to access, rectify, restrict processing, or request complete deletion of your personal and biometric history, please contact our dedicated DPO directly at:
