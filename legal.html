Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 January 2024

1. Introduction

Health Haven (Pty) Ltd ("Health Haven", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our website, mobile application, and clinical services (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, national identity number, and residential address.
  • Health Information: Biometric data (weight, heart rate, blood pressure, sleep patterns), medical history, symptoms, and clinical notes.
  • Device Information: Data from connected health devices (Withings smart scales, watches, and other compatible wearables).
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, app usage patterns, and website interaction data.
  • Corporate Data: For B2B clients, employee IDs and aggregated health metrics (individual health data is never shared with employers).

3. Data Aggregation Protocols

Health Haven employs clinical-grade data aggregation protocols to ensure the integrity and utility of your health information:

  • All biometric data is synchronised from FDA/CE-approved devices via encrypted API connections.
  • Data is normalised against clinically validated reference ranges specific to Southern African populations.
  • Trend analysis is performed using rolling 30-day, 90-day, and 12-month windows to identify clinically significant patterns.
  • Aggregated workforce health reports for B2B clients are anonymised using k-anonymity protocols (k>=5) before any summary statistics are generated.

4. Zero Third-Party Selling

Health Haven does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We do not monetise user data through advertising networks, data brokers, or any other commercial arrangement. Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Providing clinical services and health monitoring
  • Generating personal health insights for your benefit
  • Quality improvement and clinical research (using fully anonymised datasets only)
  • Legal compliance and regulatory reporting as required by law

5. Biometric Encryption

Your biometric and health data is protected using the following encryption standards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses TLS 1.3 with AES-256-GCM.
  • Encryption at Rest: All stored health data is encrypted using AES-256 with keys managed through AWS KMS Hardware Security Modules (HSMs).
  • End-to-End Encryption: Clinician-patient messaging uses end-to-end encryption; even Health Haven cannot read the content of your clinical conversations.
  • Key Rotation: Encryption keys are rotated every 90 days in accordance with ISO 27001 standards.

6. Data Retention

Personal health data is retained for the duration of your active account plus 7 years following account closure, as required by Botswana medical records regulations. After this period, data is securely deleted using NIST 800-88 compliant sanitisation methods.

7. Your Rights

Under the DPA 2024, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to medical retention requirements)
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@healthhaven.co.bw.

8. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@healthhaven.co.bw or write to: Health Haven (Pty) Ltd, Plot 50371, Fairgrounds Office Park, Gaborone, Botswana.

DPA 2024 Compliance Statement

Botswana Data Protection Act, 2024

Health Haven (Pty) Ltd is fully committed to compliance with the Botswana Data Protection Act 2024 (Act No. [XX] of 2024). This statement outlines our data protection practices, breach reporting procedures, and cross-border data transfer compliance.

1. Data Protection Officer

Health Haven has appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) responsible for overseeing our data protection strategy and ensuring compliance with the DPA 2024:

  • Name: Ms. Lesedi Kgosietsile
  • Email: dpo@healthhaven.co.bw
  • Phone: +267 31 000 000
  • Address: Plot 50371, Fairgrounds Office Park, Gaborone, Botswana
  • Office Hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 - 17:00 CAT

The DPO can be contacted directly for any data protection concerns, subject access requests, or breach notifications.

2. 72-Hour Breach Reporting

In accordance with Section 58 of the DPA 2024, Health Haven maintains a strict breach notification protocol:

  • Detection: All potential data breaches are detected through automated monitoring systems and manual security reviews.
  • Assessment: Upon detection, our Security Team assesses the breach within 4 hours to determine the scope and risk to data subjects.
  • Notification to Authority: If the breach poses a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, we notify the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
  • Notification to Data Subjects: Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of affected individuals, we communicate directly with those individuals without undue delay.
  • Documentation: All breaches are documented regardless of whether notification is required, including the facts, effects, and remedial actions taken.

3. Section 74 Cross-Border Compliance

Under Section 74 of the DPA 2024, Health Haven complies with cross-border data transfer requirements as follows:

  • Primary Data Storage: All personal data is stored within the Republic of Botswana, in ISO 27001 certified data centres located in Gaborone.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Our primary cloud provider (AWS) operates an Africa (South Africa) Region. Where data transit through South African nodes occurs, appropriate safeguards including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and encryption in transit are implemented.
  • Third-Party Processors: Any third-party service provider that may process personal data outside Botswana is subject to a Data Processing Agreement that includes DPA 2024 compliant safeguards.
  • Data Localisation: Biometric and health data classified as "sensitive personal data" under the DPA 2024 is strictly localised within Botswana and is not transferred across borders under any circumstances.
  • Adequacy Decisions: We only transfer personal data to jurisdictions that have been deemed adequate by the Botswana Information Commissioner or where appropriate safeguards have been implemented.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Health Haven processes personal data under the following lawful bases as set out in Section 27 of the DPA 2024:

  • Consent: Explicit consent is obtained for the processing of sensitive health data.
  • Contractual Necessity: Processing necessary for the performance of our service agreement with you.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing necessary for compliance with medical records and public health regulations.
  • Vital Interests: Processing necessary to protect your vital interests in emergency situations.

5. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Pseudonymisation and encryption of personal data
  • Ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of processing systems
  • Regular testing, assessing, and evaluating the effectiveness of security measures
  • Annual third-party security audits and penetration testing
  • Staff training on data protection and information security

6. Registration

Health Haven (Pty) Ltd is registered as a data controller and data processor with the Botswana Information Commissioner's Office. Registration number: DPC/BW/2024/001234.

Medical Disclaimer

Please Read Carefully

Important Notice

Health Haven is a wellness and health management platform, not a medical diagnosis or emergency service. The information, guidance, and services provided through our platform are intended for general wellness purposes only and should not be construed as medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

1. Not a Substitute for Professional Medical Care

The Services provided by Health Haven, including health tracking, AI-generated insights, and clinician consultations, are designed to complement — not replace — the relationship between you and your primary healthcare provider. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

2. Emergency Situations

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 997 immediately (Botswana Emergency Medical Services) or proceed to the nearest hospital emergency department. Do not rely on Health Haven for emergency medical assistance. Our platform is not monitored for emergencies and clinicians are not available for emergency consultation 24/7.

3. AI-Generated Insights

Any health insights, recommendations, or alerts generated by our artificial intelligence systems are based on pattern recognition and population-level data. These insights are informational only and do not constitute a clinical diagnosis. Always verify AI-generated recommendations with a licensed clinician before taking action.

4. Clinician Consultations

Telemedicine consultations through Health Haven are provided by licensed Botswana Health Professions Council (BHPC) registered clinicians. However, telemedicine has limitations and may not be appropriate for all medical conditions. Your clinician will advise if an in-person examination is required.

5. Accuracy of Data

Health Haven relies on data you provide and data from connected devices. The accuracy of insights and recommendations depends on the accuracy and completeness of this data. Ensure all devices are properly calibrated and report any discrepancies to our support team.

6. No Guarantee of Results

Individual results from using Health Haven's Services may vary. We do not guarantee specific health outcomes, weight loss, disease reversal, or the prevention of any medical condition. Health outcomes depend on many factors including genetics, adherence to recommendations, and individual physiology.

7. Regulatory Compliance

Health Haven (Pty) Ltd is registered with the Botswana Health Professions Council and operates in compliance with the Medical Act (Cap 44:03) and the Public Health Act (Cap 63:01). Our clinicians are independently licensed by the BHPC and maintain their own professional liability insurance.

8. Contact for Medical Concerns

If you have questions about the appropriateness of Health Haven for your specific health situation, please consult your primary care physician before registering for our Services.
